## [GDR CoPhy - June 2026](/assets/files/gdr_cophy_2026.pdf)
Presentation akin to the previous one (LSST France, May 2026), with additional results on mass and concentration extraction biases obtained via mimicking current analysis hypothesis. Fitting spherical NFW profiles over known non spherical NFW lenses can lead to biases of about 30% on mass and concentration estimation. Presented to GDR CoPhy at LPCA.

## [LSST France - May 2026](/assets/files/lsst_france_may2026.pdf)
Presenting advancements on _excalibur_ regarding code development and architecture, as well as the theoretical framework and code validation. First results about lens geometry effects on the shear distribution measured by _excalibur_. Presented to LSST collaboration at LAPP.

## [LSST France - June 2025](/assets/files/LSST_France_2025.pdf)
Presenting the first steps towards measuring some observables on dark matter halos' shapes and masses, as well as some theoretical context. Presented to LSST collaboration at IJCLab.

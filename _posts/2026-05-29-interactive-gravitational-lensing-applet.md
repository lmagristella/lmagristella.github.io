I added an interactive educational applet to visualize **gravitational lensing**.

This tool performs **ray tracing** of light propagation through different lens geometries, with approximately **1,000,000 photons simulated** for each configuration.

## Features

The applet includes five distinct **NFW lens** configurations:

- one spherical mass distribution;
- three prolate lenses with different orientations relative to the line of sight;
- one arbitrarily aligned triaxial lens.

Each configuration is designed to highlight different **weak/strong lensing** effects, such as shear anisotropy, caustic formation, and projection effects.

## Notes

- Linux only for now;
- mainly intended for educational and demonstrative purposes;
- approximately **1,000,000 photons** simulated per run.
